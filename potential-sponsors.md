# MD Inverter — Potential Ad Sponsors Research

**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Audience:** Professionals who use AI chatbots, content creators, writers, developers who use AI for content generation
**Pain point:** AI output formatting is annoying — users who clip AI text into docs/emails/slack

---

## Sponsor Criteria

- Relevant to audience (AI tools, writing tools, productivity tools)
- Active marketing budget (not pre-revenue startups)
- B2C or prosumer product (not enterprise-only)
- Willing to do direct sponsorship (not through an ad network)
- Ad-friendly (no controversy, clear value prop)

## Outreach Strategy

### Cold Email Template

```
Subject: Sponsorship opportunity — MD Inverter (AI text cleanup tool)

Hi [Name],

I built MD Inverter — a tool that converts AI-generated markdown into 
clean, natural text. It's a simple web app that thousands of AI users 
will find useful.

I'm looking for 2-3 partners to sponsor the web app with small banner 
ads. The audience is exactly your target market: professionals who use 
AI for writing but hate the formatting.

I'm thinking $300-500/month for a permanent banner placement. Clean 
design, non-intrusive, relevant audience. Happy to work with your 
existing creative or mine one for you.

Would you be interested in a quick chat?

Best,
D
```

### Warm Outreach Channels

1. **Product Hunt** — Check if any products have launched recently
2. **Twitter/X** — Search for "AI writing tool" or "copywriting" mentions
3. **Hacker News** — Products that have HN posts are open to community building
4. **Indie Hackers** — Many AI startups are here actively marketing
5. **GitHub** — Search for "AI writing" repos, many have marketing teams

### Key Contacts

- Marketing/Partnerships emails are usually: `partnerships@company.com`, `sponsorships@company.com`, `hello@company.com`
- For smaller startups, direct founder outreach on Twitter/X works well
- For larger companies (Jasper, Grammarly), use their general contact + mark for "partnerships"

## Pricing Guide

| Sponsor Size | Banner Size | Monthly Price | What's Included |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Micro (startup) | 200x200 pushpin | $100-200 | Banner only |
| Small (growing) | 200x200 or 468x60 | $200-400 | Banner + brief description |
| Medium (funded) | 468x60 or 970x90 | $400-800 | Banner + creative + click tracking |
| Large (mature) | 970x90 or custom | $800-1500+ | Banner + landing page + analytics |

**Target:** 3-4 sponsors at $200-500/month = $600-2000/month recurring
